Embodied Intelligence for Aerospace Robotics with Trustworthy Spatial-Temporal Autonomy

CSUN Computer Science / Embodied AGI  NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory

Project Overview

Synopsis

ERA is an early-stage research effort focused on building embodied AI systems for aerospace missions, with an emphasis on practical autonomy, robust decision-making, and trustworthy multi-agent behavior.

  • Aiming to build a general foundation model for many downstream tasks.
  • Supporting action prediction, rover navigation, and multi-agent planning.
  • Prioritizing trustworthiness and value alignment in autonomous systems.
  • Making mission operations more autonomous across changing conditions.
  • Testing in simulation with multiple rovers on lunar terrain.
